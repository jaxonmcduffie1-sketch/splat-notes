# Splat Notes: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 9, 2026_

Splat Notes ("Splat") is a notepad that turns your notes into to-dos, plans, ideas and messages. This policy explains what happens to your information.

## Your notes stay yours

- Everything you write in Splat is stored on your device. Splat has no servers that receive your notes, and nobody at Splat can read them.
- Suggestions and drafted messages are made **on your device**, by simple rules or by Apple Intelligence's on-device model. Your notes aren't sent anywhere to make them.
- **iCloud sync** is off unless you turn it on (Settings → Sync with iCloud). When it's on, your notes are kept in **your own private iCloud account** so they appear on your iPhone, iPad and Mac, under Apple's privacy terms. We can't see them there.

## Calendars and Contacts

- With your permission, Splat reads your calendars to show your day and find free times, and adds the plans you choose to the calendar you pick. It never sends invitations.
- With your permission, Splat looks up a contact's name and phone number when you tap a "Text …" or "Call …" label, so you can message or call them. Messages and calls go through Apple's Messages and Phone apps. Splat never sends anything itself.
- This information stays on your device.

## Notifications

If you turn them on, Splat schedules reminders (a morning summary, deadline reminders and a daily sort reminder) on your device. They are made from your notes on your device; nothing is sent to us.

## Purchases

Splat+ is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment. We receive no card details and no personal information from your purchase.

## What we collect

Nothing. Splat has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## Children

Splat is suitable for everyone and doesn't collect information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If Splat ever sends anything else off your device, you'll be asked first, and this policy will say exactly what is sent.
