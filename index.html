Common questions

How does Splat sort my notes?

Write one thought per line. Splat suggests where each line goes: To-do, Schedule, Idea or Contact. Tap the suggestion to accept it, or open Sort to go through everything at once.

Where are my notes stored?

On your device. Nobody at Splat can see them. If you turn on Settings → Sync with iCloud, they also sync through your own iCloud to your iPhone, iPad and Mac.

How do I connect Google or Outlook calendars?

Add the account in your device's Settings → Calendar → Accounts (on Mac: System Settings → Internet Accounts). Splat then shows those calendars and can add plans to them.

"Text Sam back" didn't find Sam.

Allow Contacts access when Splat asks, or turn it on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Contacts. Then tap the "Text Sam" label again.

I'm not getting reminders.

Open Splat's Settings → Notifications and turn them on, then check that notifications for Splat are allowed in your device's Settings → Notifications.

What does Splat+ add?

Smart suggestions on every line, Find a time in your calendar, and messages written for you. It starts with a 7-day free trial.

How do I cancel Splat+?

In Splat: Settings → Manage subscription. Or in your device's Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Apple handles all billing; refunds are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I switched phones and Splat+ is gone.

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then tap Settings → Restore purchases in Splat.
